## **Comparative Analysis of AI Model Access Providers (Normalized to a $10 Budget)**

This analysis evaluates subscription-based AI access plans within a tight $10 monthly budget. Several providers offer entry-level subscriptions at this price point, and some deliver surprising value.

> **Scope:** Only providers with recurring monthly subscriptions are included. Pay-as-you-go API providers are not listed here — see the [Free Rankings](free-rankings.md) for those.

Here is the ranking from **best value for money (Best) to least capability per dollar (Worst)**.

### **🥇 1. OpenCode Go (Subscription)**

* **The Model Class:** Open & Proprietary Flagships (*Grok 4.5, Kimi K3, GLM-5.2, MiniMax M3, DeepSeek V4 Pro, GPT 5.6 Luna, Qwen3.7 Plus, Hy3, MiMo-V2.5, DeepSeek V4 Flash*)
* **What you get:** Best Value: $10/month ($5 first month) with generous per-model request limits.
* **Limits:** Per-5-hour request limits by model; examples: 120 Grok 4.5, 110 Kimi K3, 880 GLM-5.2, 3,200 MiniMax M3, 3,450 DeepSeek V4 Pro, 4,100 GPT 5.6 Luna (2x promo), 4,300 Qwen3.7 Plus/Hy3, 30,100 MiMo-V2.5, 31,650 DeepSeek V4 Flash.
* **Limit Vulnerability:** Per-model request caps reset every 5 hours.
* **Why it's #1:** OpenCode Go still fits exactly into the $10 budget, now advertised as $5 for the first month and then $10/month. The source now lists per-model requests per 5-hour window instead of a dollar-denominated value cap.

### **🥈 2. Xiaomi MiMo (Token Plan Lite)**

* **The Model Class:** Open Flagships (*MiMo-V2.5 Pro*)
* **What you get:** Great: Frontier model + 1M context for $6.
* **Limits:** 4.1B monthly credits covering full MiMo-V2.5 model family
* **Limit Vulnerability:** Smaller credit pool (4.1B/mo).
* **Why it's #2:** At just $6, you get access to a genuine frontier model with a 1-million-token context window. The MiMo-V2.5 Pro rivals models that cost significantly more on other platforms. The Lite plan's 4.1B credit pool is smaller than the Standard plan's 11B, but for moderate use it's more than enough. And you still have $4 left over.

### **🥉 3. OpenAI (ChatGPT Go)**

* **The Model Class:** Proprietary Fast Models (*GPT-5.2 Instant*)
* **What you get:** Solid: 10x free tier, file uploads, image gen.
* **Limits:** ~100 messages per 5 hours
* **Limit Vulnerability:** Ads included; not full flagship model.
* **Why it's #3:** For $8, you get roughly 10x the free tier — approximately 100 messages with GPT-5.2 Instant per 5-hour window, plus file uploads, image generation, and an expanded context window. GPT-5.2 Instant is fast and capable for everyday tasks. The catch: OpenAI is rolling out ads in the Go tier.

### **4. Grok (SuperGrok Lite)**

* **The Model Class:** Social & Search Flagships (*Grok 4*)
* **What you get:** Limited: Fast responses, but basic capabilities only.
* **Limits:** Basic capabilities only; flagship reasoning requires $30/mo
* **Limit Vulnerability:** Flagship reasoning locked behind $30/mo tier.
* **Why it's #4:** The Lite tier gives you Grok's speed and personality, but limits you to basic capabilities. The actual flagship reasoning engine (Grok 4.3 Standard) is locked behind a $30/month tier. For $10, you get a fast, responsive chatbot that's good for casual questions — but it lacks deep reasoning and coding capabilities.
